*Personal website, 4 February 2019.*

## I like design that mixes rigour and poetry.

I was an architect and software developer. After tussling with social inclusion and housing typology as an undergraduate, I started exploring my desire for creating independent self-sustaining spaces.

My last work was built at Burning Man ’18, and before that I designed for an off-grid commune called *The Singularity* in peri-urban Bangalore.

## Contents

1. [Pod Mahal](/pod-mahal/) — Multi-tiered hexagonal housing at Burning Man ’18.
2. [The Singularity](/the-singularity/) — A vision for a self-sustaining, tech-futuristic commune on the peri-urban edges of Bangalore.
3. [Pipe Dream for Kathputli Basti](/kathputli-basti/) — A response to high-rise proposals for informal settlements.
4. [Whom Do I Shift? (A Lament)](/delhi-an-inclusive-city/) — A conference paper for “Delhi: An Inclusive City?”, held at SPA Delhi in 2014.
5. **Mixed-Use Housing** — A housing typology developed for the upcoming parts of Delhi being activated by the Metro.

The last item existed as this short description on the Cargo home page; the site did not contain a separate project page for it.
